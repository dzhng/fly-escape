# Spatial response through the full Attempt owner

Scratch firing-based decoder plus reversed source bindings, exact patch and executed probe included. Cold fields/brains, real graph, one source a metre to either side, gain two, 150 ticks, normal locomotion transitions and timed life policy. Thirty paired seeds for each source side, source kind and initial walking/flying mode: 480 attempts. All finished at the horizon without errors or escapes through the distant exit. No fans, physical food, motor injection or changed Body code.

Attractive odor reduced final source distance relative to same-source/no-current controls by about2.23m on the left and0.94m on the right in both entry modes. Repellent odor increased it by0.03m on the left (interval includes zero) and0.23m on the right. Root's `summary.json` derives descriptive t(29) intervals from the preserved raw records; the delegate used normal intervals, which are not copied here.

This is directional approach, not guaranteed arrival: closest-distance improvement is much smaller, and the two initial modes soon mix through normal takeoff/landing. The asymmetric effects are accepted for further gameplay work; the user does not require equal strength. The delegate attributed asymmetry to the graph, but this fixture alone does not isolate graph membership from encoder/input asymmetry. No biological attribution is established.

The delegate reports three repeated builds/runs. The preserved final480 records are one dataset, not independent replications. Future work should not rerun identical samples without a new failure or hypothesis. No production change was integrated by this experiment.
