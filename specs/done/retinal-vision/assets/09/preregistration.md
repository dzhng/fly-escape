# Fixed-input chromatic diagnostic protocol

This protocol was written before inspecting neural responses. It inherits the spatial experiment's graph, map, exact seed/state schedule, 60 warmup plus 100 measured ticks, gain 3, endpoint exclusions and paired Bonferroni method. It tests the frozen source-informed Tm2 brightness/Tm20 blue proxy; it does not establish biological color perception, UV sensitivity, navigation or a preferred turn.

The two primary contrasts swap the existing slice-05 pair between its jointly supported upper/lower location in each eye, first at RGB8 A=[100,100,100], B=[35,117,123], then at the exact integer double A=[200,200,200], B=[70,234,246]. Both levels are inside the declared RGB gamut. RGB quantization occurs before the actual native adapter. Check per-sample diagnostic linear luminance and all Tm2 currents within 1e-12, and per-eye/channel total current within 1e-9. Preserve separate requested and effective dose reports. The swapped colors counterbalance both eyes and change location at matched total channel dose, rather than treating a uniformly larger blue dose as the primary proof.

For each intensity, run normal/swapped inputs, their chromatic-off variants (Tm20 current zero, Tm2 untouched), their all-input-silenced variants, and a fixed within-eye/channel cyclic row-current permutation. The latter preserves dose but changes spatial registration; it is descriptive. Also run the shared grayscale control with both locations at A, an independently executed identical-byte repeat, and uniform A/B fields. Uniform fields are diagnostic because total blue-channel dose changes. No RGB-to-UV transform or new population is introduced.

The normal/swapped contrasts are the two primary tests, across every frozen downstream path endpoint. Each needs a corrected voltage interval wholly beyond ±1e-9 model-voltage units. The Bonferroni family includes voltage and secondary spike-count measures across both contrasts and every frozen endpoint; spike-count results are reported separately without changing the voltage-primary ordering. The voltage floor separates numerical residue from a modeled response and is not a physiological significance threshold. Require brightness/dose checks and disappearance of stimulus-dependent differences under chromatic-off and all-input silencing. Under chromatic-off, brightness current may differ by at most 1e-12 from floating-point evaluation of equal luminance; require identical full downstream voltage/spike/refractory trajectories and spike counts; identical RGB bytes and seeds require exact full trajectory equality. Report downstream spike totals and every prespecified seed/contrast, including negative findings. A failed color gate requires explicit reslicing; scalar-brightness completion is not a substitute.

This is an offline supplied-byte experiment. The machine freeze binds original RGB byte files, graph/annotation/map/profile/color identities, source hashes and the complete condition list before Brain execution. Complete browser pose-to-tick and record provenance remain separate integration work.
