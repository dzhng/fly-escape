## Verdict and decision budget

Resource failure requires reslicing, not quality changes during a run or silently removing color. Delegate routine integration fixes within frozen ownership and tests. Preserve room geometry/timers, sixteen flies, 2/5/10 stars, progress, arrangements, odor/taste, body/decoder and About. Report changed trajectories without claiming unchanged difficulty. Use review/close-spec after shipping and update the next-agent handoff before ending.
