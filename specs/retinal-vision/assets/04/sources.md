## Finite alternatives investigated

1. **Tm20 pale/yellow receptor-specific channels:** the physiology paper discusses potentially distinct subclasses but the retained annotation supplies no per-cell receptor assignment. Inferring a split from body ordering or desired behavior would invent anatomy. Rejected.
2. **Tm20 plus broad Tm5c:** the physiology paper reports broad visible sensitivity and roughly linear Tm5c responses. The MaleCNS canonical/flywire labels agree, but source column coordinates are absent. It needs a separate source-supported anatomical registration investigation before it can receive spatial retinal input. No guessed positions or copied Tm20 positions.
3. **Fit the authors' processed response data:** [the authors' GitLab repository](https://gitlab.com/rbehnialab/chreyesees) was inspected at `91cf92581d2abaea72b96a994d69ed6d83ae05f9`. Its `data/compiled_data.parquet` contains receptor-coordinate response summaries, not RGB-response pairs. Reconstructing a stimulus spectrum and its background/coordinate transforms is a further receptor-modeling task; fitting these columns as RGB would be unjustified. No author code/data was copied into this artifact. [The raw dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/10720630) is a concrete future reconstruction source, not downloaded or claimed replicated here.

The chosen reduced model answers the user's required color-sensitivity question within the retained annotated graph. It does not claim the other visible or UV dimensions are biologically absent. A negative downstream result must reopen the model or anatomy slice explicitly, not tune these coefficients to improve escaping.
