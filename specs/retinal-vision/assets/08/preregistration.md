# Fixed-input spatial/brightness diagnostic protocol

This protocol was written before running any retinal neural experiment. It is preparatory native evidence for slice 08, not proof of the complete browser capture → transaction → recorded body chain. The retained graph, LIF model, map, coefficients, exposure and gain remain frozen. No seed, gain, population or axis search is permitted.

Each condition starts a fresh `Brain` from its native zero state and `Brain::seed_for_fly(seed, 0)`. Seeds are 1–6 for this bounded diagnostic panel; a separately frozen confirmation may use 100–129, with no change to the endpoints or adapter. Run 60 unforced warmup ticks, then 100 measured ticks holding the exact supplied RGB8 image fixed. Native tick here means one `Brain::step`, as in the existing neural-vision analyzer. There is no body movement, optical feedback or preferred motor sign in this experiment.

The primary population is the sorted union of non-input, non-motor nodes on every retained map cell's audited relay/readout path, checked against the actual directed graph. Exclude all 1,176 Tm2/Tm20 input indices, including zero-current cells, and every native motor/readout index. No responsive-cell selection occurs. Primary endpoint: each cell's mean measured voltage. Apply the existing analyzer's two-sided paired Bonferroni 95% intervals over two measures (voltage and spikes) times every prespecified contrast times every primary cell. Voltage intervals must lie wholly beyond ±1e-9 model-voltage units; this numerical floor is three orders above the adapter-equality tolerance and is not a physiological effect threshold. Every contrast needs at least one corrected voltage interval beyond that floor to pass this diagnostic panel. Spike-count intervals are a separately preregistered secondary endpoint with a zero-count floor, reported without changing the voltage-primary ordering: preserve per-primary-cell counts by seed, complete reachable non-input/non-motor counts for every seed and summed over seeds, per-seed population totals and active-cell counts. Full voltage/spike/refractory trajectory hashes provide exact repeat controls. A corrected voltage response without a corrected spike-count response is labeled subthreshold at the observed endpoint. Motor readouts are descriptive only.

Primary RGB fixtures have the Tm20 branch zeroed after the frozen adapter, leaving Tm2 brightness intact. Use dark, uniform gray 64/128/255 and four gray-128 single-sample spots at the pre-existing joint-support locations L163, L562, R170 and R567. Those locations were selected in slice 05 from geometry and support before neural responses were measured. The seven primary contrasts are gray64−dark, gray128−gray64, white−gray128, Lupper−Rupper, Llower−Rlower, Lupper−Llower and Rupper−Rlower. Assert actual mapped spot doses agree to 1e-9. Labels upper/lower refer to frozen image rows, not a calibrated animal visual field.

Controls include an independently executed identical-byte repeat, zeroed visual current, all-input silencing under both dark and bright input, and a fixed within-eye/channel cyclic permutation of current destinations. The permutation preserves total current and input populations while disrupting row-to-neuron registration; it is an explicit experimental ablation, not a replacement map. It is descriptive, not expected to annihilate neural differences.

Retain every supplied scene/pose capture present in the frozen input pack and the four 128×128/radius-15 quality captures as descriptive native-input conditions, with original bytes, hashes and available pose/scene metadata. They must not be reclassified as controlled comparisons merely because they differ. If fixed-pose open/blocked RGB fixtures arrive before freeze, add their floor and flight contrasts as separate primary occlusion comparisons with identical background/pose and Tm20 off; otherwise leave the physical-occlusion gate pending. Freezing creates a write-once machine manifest binding the exact input files, conditions, contrasts, source hashes, graph/map/profile identities, parameters and endpoints. A started marker prevents silently rerunning a held-out directory.

An absent or inconsistent response is a failed or insufficient diagnostic result, not permission to change gain, axes, seeds or model coefficients. Native input proof alone cannot close slice 08. Camera invariance, transaction/record correspondence and actual body observations remain integration gates.
