What counts as color vision here?

The final signal retains color, and different colors at matched brightness must produce repeatable downstream neural differences. Colorful panels feeding only brightness do not pass. The RGB approximation is declared; no UV is invented from artwork.

What happens to an old replay?

A clear unsupported-recording message and a usable way to start again. No crash, no migration, and no damage to saved campaign progress or object arrangements.

One owner for the world, eye profile, simulation tick, input mapping and archive. Eye panels only read recorded samples. Every visual implementation slice ends with comparison where relevant and an unprimed screenshot critique.
