The fan replaces the freestanding oval with directional silk between a wall and floor. The first-house mounting uses the rendered wall face: centre x4.8, thickness 0.12 m, interior face x4.74. ## Evidence and verdict

`before/` is the original. `rejected-first/` preserves the first oval candidate. `second/` preserves the lower-anchor revision, rejected as insufficient overall attachment. `third/` contains the retained fan: whole, close and extra-zoom production setup views, matching 4× crops, thumbnail and zero-error browser report. [The capture harness](capture.mjs) repeats camera gestures after world readiness. [Pixel telemetry](pixel-diff.json) confirms changed feature regions; it does not establish quality or identical camera/wind timing.

The independent reviewer found the fan worth keeping: the triangle removes the oval, its right edge meets trim, and directional strands read as a corner web at extra zoom. No obvious new clipping or spider occlusion was found. This reviewer had inspected source before the final images, so that judgment was not strictly unprimed.

A fresh-agent spawn was rejected by the thread limit. Root therefore used the screenshot-critique skill's documented no-subagent adversarial fallback, inspecting all 18 before/second/third full images and crops. Its strongest objections were:

- Whole frame: the web still dissolves into a few specks.
- Close frame: broken, dotted silk makes the structure hard to parse.
- Extra zoom: left endpoints still read as suspended over the floor, and the upper anchor disappears with the wall.
- Spider legs remain rigid and angular.

After those objections, root accepted the bounded composition improvement: the triangular fan and right-base contact are clearly better than the freestanding oval. This does not close overall readability or attachment acceptance. The room-scale gate remains unresolved.

## Review and choices

Shape review retained one source/export/contact path and replaced the oval instead of layering another web over it. Diff review found no actionable source or physical issue. Documentation records the remaining art gate without claiming completion. The delegated composition choice is an asymmetric fan; the delegated placement choice is the actual interior wall face. Silk radii, physical envelope, placement policy and simulation architecture remain unchanged. These decisions improve mounting without using thicker silk or invisible collision to conceal the visual limitation.
