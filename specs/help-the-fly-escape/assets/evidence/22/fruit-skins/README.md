# Fruit skin material pass

Apple and banana retain their original authored triangles and metre dimensions. Blender vertex colours supply red/yellow apple variation and yellow banana skin with brown terminals; material roughness and coating stay in the exported assets. Both regenerated contact JSON files are byte-identical to the committed geometry. The exact rendered-apple loader and both contact-export regressions pass (14 focused tests total).

The Chrome WebGPU capture uses the exported assets in an isolated asset-lab page, not a Blender render. Fresh visual review confirms readable silhouettes and colours but identifies a smooth/plastic banana finish, apple stripe regularity and ambiguous apple identity without a stem. These are remaining art limitations; no decorative geometry was added to bypass the contact contract. The material pass is recognizable and coloured, not final photorealistic skin.
