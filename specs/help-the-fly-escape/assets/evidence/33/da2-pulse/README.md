# DA2 retention and neural pulse feasibility

This scratch experiment asks whether the annotated DA2 sensory population can be retained and whether stimulating it changes its relay activity. It does not test geosmin concentration, avoidance, or a game object. The production graph is unchanged.

Adding DA2 to the existing seed selection retained all48 annotated sensory neurons and all10 DA2 projection neurons within70000 total neurons. The original extractor reproduced the production graph hash first. The modified extraction exchanges71 ranked neighbors and adds2160 net edges; its full difference and source-identified manifest are retained here. This is a new reduced graph, not simply an input substitution on an identical circuit.

The native Brain received current2 on all48 DA2 sensory neurons during steps400–599 of1000, compared with no current using identical seeds0,1,2. It contains no field or moving body. Seven sensory neurons have unknown side; all are included in this bilateral perturbation, reported separately rather than assigned a side. Stimulated cells do not overlap motor readouts. Before-pulse observations match exactly. Root independently reran the compiled probe and reproduced every observation and paired difference, excluding elapsed time.

During stimulation, mean active-minus-control spike fractions increase approximately0.487 at the sensory population,0.043 at left projection neurons and0.056 at right projection neurons. Current turning-group differences are small and vary across seeds. The injected population approaches the current model’s refractory firing ceiling, making this a strong uncalibrated perturbation, not a physical dose. Propagation into projection neurons is established under these conditions; three seeds and bilateral stimulation cannot establish directional behavior, a particular loss of information, or where any behavioral limitation originates. Root rejects the draft interpretation that propagation stops exactly one synapse downstream.

[Stensmyr et al. (2012)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23217715/) supports the biological Or56a/DA2–geosmin association. It does not calibrate this current or reduced network. [Lüdke et al. (2025)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ejn.70036) provides receptor concentration-response measurements, but calcium responses are not automatically firing rates. [Shiu et al. (2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07763-9) validates specific sensorimotor tasks with its own model and explicitly limits interpretation; its results cannot validate our different dynamics and locomotion decoder by association.

The native pulse ran in about five seconds. Reproduction uses `extract_da2.py` with the root source dataset, then `probe.rs` as a scratch sim example against that output directory. The scripts retain their original absolute scratch paths. No runtime schema or gameplay changes were adopted.
