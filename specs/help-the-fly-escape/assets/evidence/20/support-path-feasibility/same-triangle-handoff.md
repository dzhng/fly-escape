# Same-triangle vertex handoff

**A conditional first handoff succeeds; no runtime adoption.** Starting from a
query-discovered face/vertex, a failed cone certificate proposes one neighbor from
the original hull edge incidence. The path is the maximum of those two continuous
face-height expressions. No event timestamp is selected or rounded into place.

Every excluded hull vertex must be dominated by at least one active vertex over
the entire interval. Both possible witnesses must remain in the triangle. Thus
the winning chart provides actual contact; at equality the triangle also contains
the intervening contact edge. Every other apple triangle still needs an interval
exclusion/separation certificate. Earlier certified prefixes cannot change when a
neighbor is added: their original cone proof already bounded that neighbor below
the original chart. Adjacency limits proposals and replaces none of these proofs.

[Results](same-triangle-handoff-results.json) show complete turn0.45→0.51 radians
and tilt-up-X0→0.43 paths with opposite strict endpoint height-difference signs,
proving at least one real winner crossover (not uniqueness). Twenty-run native
means are4.83ms and18.01ms; these costs are not accepted for campaign updates.

Wider paths remain unresolved at a third vertex, competitor work exhaustion, or
the original witness leaving its triangle. That last failure can concern an
obsolete losing witness; it does not establish actual support loss. A future
certificate could exempt a witness only after proving it cannot win on that
interval. No sampled loser removal or backward path mutation is justified here.

Independent review found no new false certificate. The preceding retained-chart
scope still applies: exact rounded-mm geometry, sole apple, declared normalized
axis-angle path, approximate initial query match, fixed positive face denominator.
Endpoint conversion and authoritative caller-root preservation are not proven;
rotating edge/face charts, other-food scenes and body/replay ownership remain open.
