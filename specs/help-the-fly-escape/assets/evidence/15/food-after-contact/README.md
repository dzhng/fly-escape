The contact fixed-point repair is included in every arm. These are twenty-fly, ten-minute attempts on the authored first house, with the production neural graph and unchanged tuning. Exit suction is identical and local; no fan is placed. Seeds are paired, not independent evidence for each condition.

The entrance is several metres from the opening. The exit-side position was placed through the actual browser UI; the saved coordinates and screenshot are included. Both configurations retain fixed household objects. “Empty” means no player-added objects, not an unfurnished or cue-free house.

Moving food nearer the escape improves the mean relative to the entrance placement, but neither arrangement improves the mean over the empty control. This is a small tuning comparison, not a held-out acceptance result or proof of chemical-specific attraction. Every fly that approached the entrance banana subsequently left its measured neighborhood, so these runs do not show permanent retention there. Proximity and room residence cannot distinguish neural attraction from incidental passage by themselves.
