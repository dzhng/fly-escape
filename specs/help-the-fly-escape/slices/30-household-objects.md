## Passes and gates

- Fixed-object ownership: level-authored placements share effect resolution with editable placements, persist in setup/replay, reserve their footprint and do not consume inventory. Verify atomic edits, stable effect ordering and browser visibility. Enforce the one-fan campaign inventory.
- Distinct sensory objects: enable and measure the relevant neural pathways before presenting attraction/repulsion as useful. Preserve cold random starts and actual neural outputs. Source naming must distinguish biological observations from game modeling.
- Household integration: native clothes, shoes, dishes and pet context accompany actual fixed effects; native zapper and spider web accompany visible avoidable hazards. Visible geometry, contact and terminal labels agree. Source art is not production acceptance; inspect actual house framing and follow zoom.
- Campaign redesign: use fixed competing cues, varied inventory and timed escape attempts, then run the existing paired tuning/holdout gates. Historical multi-fan successes do not satisfy this pass.

Fixed objects are authored level conditions, not a second editable inventory. Rendering consumes the same definitions that generate effects. Do not introduce a parallel steering controller or invisible oversized food/hazard regions.
