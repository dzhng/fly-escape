Choose the smallest evidence-supported representation; no gain sweep against escape score.
4. **Behavior and game interpretation — pending.** Compare supported sensory input with no stimulus, including mirrored conditions where direction is claimed. Report variability, neural propagation and body behavior separately. Lack of avoidance is not automatically a failure; lack of input grounding is. Replace generic runtime stimulus assumptions only after the evidence supports the replacement, then reassess both authored levels and star thresholds.
