# Banana contact asset

The curved body and unequal ends form one closed skin in native metres, with a grounded envelope-centre pivot. A separate overlapping stalk collider would contradict that shared surface.

[The authoring source](author.py) owns dimensions and reuses the shared house export and neutral staging helpers. The shared skin author supplies yellow colour variation, roughness and brown terminals without changing the closed mesh. The exported surface is registered as native edible food. Diagnostic body tests exercise landing and feeding on its surface. Timed campaigns retain odor and physical contact but do not use feeding or life extension.

Run `author.py` through Blender, then [validate.py](validate.py) to inspect the reimported GLB and reload the saved native scene. The [contact baker](../../../apps/asset-lab/scripts/export-contact.ts) writes [canonical contact geometry](contact.json), joining only exactly coincident positions as described in the food asset contract. This preserves closed skin across cap-normal splits and is tested through core containment, without treating topology as gameplay acceptance. The durable topology validator separately checks welded manifold edges, winding, positive volume, triangle area and nonadjacent BVH overlaps. Its 10 nm weld applies only to the temporary GLB inspection mesh; it never modifies the authored or exported asset and is not the contact export rule. Bounds remain owned by the shared exporter, while this validator checks surface integrity. It is a bounded shape check, not an exact arithmetic collision proof.

The [terminal shape evidence](../../../specs/done/help-the-fly-escape/assets/evidence/21/banana-terminal/README.md) records fixed-view comparison and the reverse blossom diagnostic. Skin material and terminal geometry remain separate authoring concerns.

The placement loader and contact baker share exact-coordinate canonicalization, so shading seams do not create competing surface identities. Runtime validation accepts the exact authored triangle count for edible assets; generic floor cues retain their smaller limit. Placed instances share native geometry rather than rescaling or simplifying contact at runtime. The placement owner defines odor and footprint independently of the mesh. Historical shape comparisons do not establish a food-guidance strategy.
