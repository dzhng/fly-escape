# Banana shape preparation

One ordinary medium household banana is assumed: approximately 22 cm long and 4 cm thick, lying on its side. The larger cut stalk/collar and smaller recessed blossom scar distinguish orientation. Both join the curved body in one closed skin; the stalk cross-section follows its bend rather than flattening at the tip. Its closed skin is a single baked surface in native metres, with a grounded envelope-centre pivot; it has no separate overlapping stalk collider.

[The authoring source](author.py) owns dimensions and reuses the shared house export and neutral staging helpers. The shared skin author now supplies yellow colour variation, roughness and brown terminals without changing the closed mesh. The exported surface is registered as native edible food. Focused body tests exercise landing and feeding on its actual surface; campaign placement and food-dependent success require separate gameplay evidence.

Run `author.py` through Blender, then [validate.py](validate.py) to inspect the reimported GLB and reload the saved native scene. The [contact baker](../../../apps/asset-lab/scripts/export-contact.ts) writes [canonical contact geometry](contact.json), joining only exactly coincident positions as described in the food asset contract. This preserves closed skin across cap-normal splits and is tested through core containment, without treating topology as gameplay acceptance. The durable topology validator separately checks welded manifold edges, winding, positive volume, triangle area and nonadjacent BVH overlaps. Its 10 nm weld applies only to the temporary GLB inspection mesh; it never modifies the authored or exported asset and is not the contact export rule. Bounds remain owned by the shared exporter, while this validator checks surface integrity. It is a bounded shape check, not an exact arithmetic collision proof.

The [terminal shape evidence](../../../specs/help-the-fly-escape/assets/evidence/21/banana-terminal/README.md) records fixed-view comparison and the reverse blossom diagnostic. Skin material and terminal geometry remain separate authoring concerns.

The placement loader and contact baker share exact-coordinate canonicalization, so shading seams do not create competing surface identities. Runtime validation accepts the exact authored triangle count for edible assets; generic floor cues retain their smaller limit. The banana has 9,680 native triangles, shared by placed instances rather than rescaled or simplified at runtime. Its new tool uses the same odor effect as apple and its own native footprint. Existing `fruit` saves retain apple identity, while the palette names the two fruits explicitly. Scent crumbs remain odor-only.
