# Household vinegar bottle

A shouldered amber bottle, broad paper label and screw cap identify a kitchen
container without miniature lettering. Label bands share the bottle surface,
so material boundaries do not introduce overlapping contact shells. The apple
mark supplies a food cue at closer views; the palette name supplies the contents
at small thumbnail sizes.

The native grounded mesh uses embedded materials and closed components. Source
strength and contact roles belong to integration; appearance does not establish
odor concentration or a biological response. Blender staging is authoring
evidence, not production acceptance.
