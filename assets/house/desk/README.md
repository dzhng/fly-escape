# Study writing desk

This closed-pedestal writing desk has a 1.4 × 0.7 metre grounded footprint and a 0.75-metre work surface. The laptop sets its catalog envelope height to 1.05 metres. Drawers, brass handles, notebook and laptop identify its study role; the full plinth prevents a visible floor passage that would contradict the existing solid footprint.

The [shared authoring source](../author-study-bedroom.py) owns native geometry and portable wood/material finishes. The grounded origin is at the footprint centre, with +Z facing the chair. The chair is a separate catalog furnishing so the floor between them remains navigable. It is intentionally a bulky writing station rather than a legged desk; fine grain, cloth texture and a realistic knee recess remain art limitations.
