# Native kitchen run

This combined kitchen furnishing occupies a grounded 2.4 × 0.65 metre footprint and reaches 2.1 metres at its left refrigerator. At zero quarter-turns, +Z is the working front and −Z is the wall side. The floor-centred origin aligns directly with the catalog solid.

The refrigerator occupies the left 0.65 metres. The remaining counter is 0.9 metres high with a four-ring hob, oven front, recessed metal sink, faucet and cupboard fronts. There are no upper cupboards over the centre/right, leaving a wall window visible above the counter. A continuous plinth closes the floor footprint; this model uses the existing single-solid contract, without a separate appliance or sink physics system.

The [authoring source](author.py) owns all geometry and portable walnut, stone, enamel and metal materials. Native export bounds are checked against the shared catalog. Surface texture, appliance controls and the shallow basin remain deliberately simplified.
