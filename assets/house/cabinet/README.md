# Closed-base cabinet

The [catalogue](../catalog.json) owns the native envelope. A grounded centre pivot, glTF +Y up and +Z front allow placement without rescaling. The closed plinth deliberately offers no apparent route beneath the cabinet, matching the physical solid.

[author.py](author.py) creates the original geometry and an isolated neutral stage. [Shared validation](../authoring.py) checks both source and imported GLB bounds; a mismatch requires re-authoring. Production materials are owned by the [house finishing pipeline](../README.md), rather than the neutral shape stage.

[Furnishing evidence](../../../specs/done/help-the-fly-escape/assets/evidence/20/furniture-identity/README.md) records loading and occupancy checks. A verified envelope alone does not establish realistic art or fly behavior.
