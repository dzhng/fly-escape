# Closed-base cabinet

Original Blender geometry, authored in metres with a grounded centre pivot, +Y up in GLB and +Z front. Envelope:1.2m wide ×0.45m deep ×0.85m tall. The closed plinth deliberately offers no apparent under-cabinet route. `author.py` produces a separate asset scene and neutral authoring stage, the GLB, .blend and three stage renders. No downloaded models or textures.

This is shape preparation only, not a production house-kit replacement or accepted furnished-game asset. Per-key loading and authoritative footprint integration belong to spec20/21; natural materials belong to22. Do not pass this metre-scale model to the old unit-solid loader or stretch it into unrelated props.
